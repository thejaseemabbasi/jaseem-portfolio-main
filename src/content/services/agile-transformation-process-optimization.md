## Methodology matched to reality

Agile transformation is about removing waste, not forcing rigid ceremonies where they do not fit. Whether a team needs strict Scrum, a Scrum/Kanban hybrid, or disciplined Waterfall, the workflow should make quality and speed easier.

## Proven in practice

- **ADGM (OVRLOD):** Enforced structured SLA and DevOps governance, cutting delivery risks by **30%**, issues by **25%**, and delivery time by **30%**.
- **MicroPets:** Executed an 8-month Waterfall build to 100% of scope on schedule, launching at **99.8% crash-free** with a **4.6★** rating.
- **LMCC:** Eliminated ad-hoc planning ambiguity across iOS, Android, and Web streams.
