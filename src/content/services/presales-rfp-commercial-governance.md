## Winning work that ships cleanly

Projects succeed or fail during scoping. Clear assumptions, realistic estimates, and disciplined baseline tracking protect trust on both sides of the contract.

## Proven in practice

- **Technyx Systems:** Authored presales RFP proposals covering feasibility, WBS, and cost/resource estimation.
- **ADGM (OVRLOD):** Governed a **$1M+ Fixed-Price SOW** and transitioned the account smoothly into an ongoing SLA-governed maintenance contract.
- **WAGMI Defense:** Structured the commercial shift from Fixed-Price MVP to an ongoing hourly T&M engagement.
