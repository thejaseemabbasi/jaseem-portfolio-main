## Two weeks, ten working days

A typical sprint pairs focused human facilitation with AI handling repetitive prep:
- **Day 1 (Sprint planning):** Agree the sprint goal and capacity; AI drafts the agenda and pulls prior sprint velocity.
- **Every day (Daily stand-up):** Spot and clear blockers the same day; AI turns notes into a short summary with owners.
- **Day 5 (Backlog refinement):** Shape the next sprint with the Product Owner; AI drafts user stories and acceptance criteria to refine together.
- **Day 8 (Release go/no-go):** Run the readiness checklist with QA, UAT, and DevOps; AI collects open risks into a release summary.
- **Day 10 (Review & Retro):** Demo shipped work, gather stakeholder feedback, and commit to one or two concrete improvements.
