## How it supports delivery

Ambitious builds rarely slip because engineers cannot code—they slip when scope, dependencies, and ownership stay vague. I take direct accountability for timelines, resource allocation, risks, and stakeholder communication from kickoff through closure.

## Proven in practice

- **Zayed National Museum:** Delivered a 7-language Sitecore CMS website and companion iOS/Android apps on schedule and under budget.
- **ADGM Multi-Site Migration:** Directed a 25+ specialist team migrating 5,000+ pages across five properties onto Optimizely DXP.
- **Direct Macro:** Completed a full legacy-to-modern e-commerce stack migration within a six-month roadmap.
