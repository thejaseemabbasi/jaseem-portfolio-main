## The challenge

[Direct Macro](https://directmacro.com/) was held back by legacy infrastructure and manual catalog updates across multiple hardware vendors. The full migration had to ship within a six-month roadmap under tight resource constraints.

## What we did

- **Managed the six-month roadmap.** Maintained strict risk registers, issue logs, and sprint prioritization across design, engineering, and QA.
- **Engineered vendor feed automation.** Designed the data-processing logic and cron jobs that fetch, normalize, and apply dynamic pricing rules from third-party vendor APIs.
- **Validated edge cases with QA.** Authored detailed test scenarios to ensure accurate catalog and checkout behavior at launch.

## What shipped

A modern, scalable e-commerce platform with automated multi-vendor catalog synchronization and dynamic pricing.
