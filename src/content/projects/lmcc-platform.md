## The challenge

Labor-management communication was fragmented across emails, calls, and paper trails. [Southern California LMCC](https://www.socallmcc.org/lmcc-app) needed a unified iOS, Android, and web hub with auditable records and clear operational reporting.

## What we did

- **Standardized PRDs and prioritization.** Replaced ad-hoc feature requests with structured requirement specs and a long-term product roadmap.
- **Designed KPI visibility.** Built a dedicated KPI dashboard tracking communication frequency and issue resolution times for enterprise clients.
- **Aligned mobile and web releases.** Coordinated engineering, UX, and QA across all three platforms to keep releases predictable.

## What shipped

A unified multi-platform communication hub that improved workflow efficiency by 70%, stakeholder alignment by 50%, and user adoption by 40%.
