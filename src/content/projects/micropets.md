## The challenge

MicroPets required a fixed-scope, high-performance hyper-casual endless runner with tight difficulty tuning and zero tolerance for schedule or budget drift.

## What we did

- **Authored the Game Design Document (GDD).** Defined core gameplay mechanics, power-ups, asset specifications, and the mathematical speed-progression curve.
- **Executed structured Waterfall governance.** Managed Requirements, Design, Implementation, Verification, and Maintenance sequentially across art, engineering, and QA.
- **Maintained transparent stakeholder reporting.** Regular milestone reviews kept expectations aligned and lifted client satisfaction by 25%.

## What shipped

Delivered 100% of predefined scope on the exact 8-month schedule, launching at a 99.8% crash-free rate and a 4.6★ App Store rating.
