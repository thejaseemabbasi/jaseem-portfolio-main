## The challenge

Launching a new 3D Free-to-Play city-builder with PvP strategy meant shipping a compelling core loop within a strict six-month MVP window while keeping 3D art production and engineering synchronized.

## What we did

- **Implemented hybrid Scrum/Kanban.** Used Scrum sprints for structured engineering features alongside a continuous Kanban pipeline for 3D art assets—lifting velocity by 25%.
- **Defined progression and PvP logic.** Designed initial game levels, balanced player progression, and validated resource-generation and PvP formulas with engineers.
- **Controlled scope and risk.** Ran MVP scoping workshops and proactively mitigated 10 critical schedule and dependency risks.

## What shipped

A complete F2P mobile strategy MVP delivered on time and within budget.
