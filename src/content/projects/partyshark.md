## The challenge

[PartyShark](https://www.cubix.co/work/party-shark/) needed two distinct experiences in one ecosystem: a detailed control dashboard for event hosts and vendors, and a fast, map-based mobile discovery experience for guests.

## What we did

- **Phased the roadmap.** Balanced host tooling depth with a frictionless guest ticket and RSVP flow.
- **Integrated payments and vendor booking.** Coordinated Stripe payment flows for cover charges, host earnings, and vendor payouts alongside an internal vendor marketplace.
- **Launched retention mechanics.** Delivered club and organization leaderboards to drive repeat community engagement.

## What shipped

A scalable mobile and web marketplace connecting hosts, guests, and local vendors within a focused six-month delivery cycle.
