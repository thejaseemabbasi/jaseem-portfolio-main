## The challenge

[Spot It](https://www.cubix.co/work/spotit/) required fast real-time location queries, reliable spot availability state, and frictionless payments between drivers and parking space owners.

## What we did

- **Led Scrum ceremonies and backlog grooming.** Kept engineering focused on iterative, testable increments aligned with core KPIs (response time and booking success).
- **Coordinated supply and demand flows.** Delivered both the driver booking experience and owner listing, pricing, and management tools.
- **Supported end-to-end QA.** Validated acceptance criteria across search, booking, and payment edge cases prior to launch.

## What shipped

A real-time peer-to-peer mobile parking marketplace delivered on schedule with scalable backend performance.
