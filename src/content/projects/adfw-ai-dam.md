## The challenge

High-profile financial events generate massive photography, video, and branded asset libraries under tight publishing windows. Manual tagging created bottlenecks, and all delivery milestones were tied directly to fixed Abu Dhabi Finance Week dates.

## What we did

- **Synchronized AI and engineering tracks.** Aligned indexing, storage, and search interface milestones under one dependency schedule.
- **Protected event deadlines.** Ran structured sprint reviews and early risk tracking so QA and UAT completed well ahead of event kickoff.

## What shipped

An enterprise AI-assisted DAM platform enabling fast asset ingestion, automated indexing, and reliable media retrieval for ADGM event teams.
