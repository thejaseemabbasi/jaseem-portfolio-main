## The challenge

[Domino Earning World (DEW)](https://dewskill2earn.com) set out to modernize classic competitive dominoes with real-time multiplayer tournaments and customizable Web3 avatars—without letting blockchain mechanics complicate core gameplay.

## What we did

- **Authored PRDs and core game logic.** Defined the rules engine and scoring formulas across Block, Draw, and All Fives domino variations.
- **Directed avatar and marketplace flows.** Collaborated with Concept and 3D art teams on customizable avatars, traits, and seamless create/rent/lease marketplace onboarding.
- **Executed a phased launch.** Shipped the free-to-play core game first to validate multiplayer stability before rolling out tournament and NFT marketplace layers.

## What shipped

A cross-platform multiplayer dominoes platform combining accessible core gameplay with player-owned avatar customization.
