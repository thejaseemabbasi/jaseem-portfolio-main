## The challenge

Flagship cultural platforms carry fixed launch dates and strict localization standards. Coordinating a seven-language Sitecore CMS website alongside companion iOS and Android applications meant keeping engineering, content specialists, and QA synchronized so translation volume never became a late-stage bottleneck.

## What we did

- **Parallelized content and engineering.** Rather than treating seven-language localization as a final step, content schemas and translation batches ran alongside core sprint milestones.
- **Unified web and mobile tracking.** Established one work breakdown structure and dependency log across Sitecore, iOS, Android, and QA streams.
- **Controlled scope early.** Clear acceptance criteria and change boundaries kept delivery predictable from early scoping through launch.

## What shipped

The [Zayed National Museum](https://zayednationalmuseum.ae/en/) digital platform launched on schedule and under budget across all seven languages and mobile platforms.
